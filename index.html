About GreenPulse

GreenPulse maps public parks, gardens, playgrounds and wooded areas across Islamabad and Rawalpindi, then pairs each location with the latest available air-quality reading from Open-Meteo, which is based on the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS).

  • Places: OpenStreetMap data retrieved through the Overpass API.
  • Air quality: Open-Meteo US AQI, PM2.5 and PM10. Readings are model-based and grouped into nearby zones to reduce requests; they are estimates for a park's area, not on-site sensor measurements, so nearby parks often show the same value.
  • Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); Copernicus CAMS data via Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0).
  • Use: Open-Meteo's free API is for non-commercial use and has published rate limits. Public data services are best-effort and may be unavailable.
